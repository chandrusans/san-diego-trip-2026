SUN NOV 22

  • 8:00 AMLeave the airport hotel. Head north on I-15.

  • EveningDinner and stars. Sam's Indian Food in Joshua Tree town is open until 8 PM on Sundays. Step outside after dark: this is a Dark Sky area.

Travel tips

Southwest

Southwest now assigns seats. On Basic fares, families get seats at check-in and may not sit together, so check your seats now. Checked bags now cost extra ($45 first, $55 second); car seats and strollers check free.

The van

A 15-seater is tall. Avoid underground garages (downtown and La Jolla); use open lots. Park nose-out and allow extra stopping distance on mountain roads to Julian and Palm Springs.

Car seats

California requires a car seat or booster for kids under 8 (or under 4'9"). Bring your own or reserve with the van.

Weather

San Diego days are mild (60s–70s °F) and evenings cool. The desert is near freezing at dawn, and the tram summit can be snowy. Pack layers and jackets for everyone.

Thanksgiving week

Mon–Wed are the quiet days, so Joshua Tree, the Zoo and SeaWorld land there. Expect crowds Fri–Sun everywhere, especially in Julian on Friday.

Fly home day

Return the van by about 2:00 PM and be at the terminal 2.5–3 hours before the 6 PM flight.

Food plan: Indian & vegetarian

Every day has a vegetarian-safe plan. San Diego's Indian hub is Miramar / Mira Mesa, about 20–25 minutes from La Mesa, where you'll find pure-vegetarian dosa and thali houses and Indian grocery stores. The closest well-rated Indian restaurant to your La Mesa stay is Himalayan Cuisine on El Cajon Blvd. Ratings come from Google or the review site named on each card.

For a group of 15

Call ahead for a table, especially on weekends. Most of these places don't take online bookings for big groups.

Strict vegetarians

Ask about egg in naan and desserts, ghee if you avoid it, and lard or chicken stock in Mexican beans and rice. Pie crusts in Julian can contain lard, so choose the vegan pies.

Theme parks

Zoo: small single-person snacks are allowed (cooler under 12"×12", no glass). SeaWorld: no outside food or picnics, so eat a big breakfast.

Stock up early

On Sunday morning, on the way to the desert, stop at an Indian grocery in Miramar (right off I-15) for theplas, parathas, khakhra, namkeen, fruit and ready meals. They cover the two desert days, where Indian food is scarce.

Day-by-day meals

SAT NOV 21

Late arrival, nothing nearby is open. Pack home-made snacks (theplas, chivda) in carry-on for the first night.

SUN NOV 22

Breakfast: San Idli in Mira Mesa, pure-veg dosas, right off I-15 on the way north. Then shop at Miramar Cash & Carry for two days of picnic food. Lunch on the mountain: Pines Café at the tram top (hummus & falafel, cheese pizza) or Peaks (vegetarian burger, mushroom ravioli). Dinner: Sam's Indian Food in Joshua Tree town (open until 8 PM on Sundays). Walmart in Yucca Valley is open till 11 PM for water and extras.

MON NOV 23

Breakfast: Frontier Café, Yucca Valley (opens 7 AM, vegetarian sandwiches; take lunch to go). Lunch: picnic inside Joshua Tree; there's no food in the park. Dinner: Himalayan Cuisine in La Mesa (Google 4.6), close to your stay.

TUE NOV 24

Zoo: carry small snacks; Lagoon Terrace is the zoo's vegetarian-tagged café. Dinner: Madurai Kitchen in Miramar, pure-veg South Indian maha thali.

WED NOV 25

Balboa Park: pack a picnic, or try the cafés along El Prado. Dinner: Sattvik Foods in Miramar, Gujarati thali with Jain dishes, about 20 minutes away. Pick up your Thanksgiving trays from Sattvik or Madras Cafe tonight. (If you choose SeaWorld: Manta Pizza margherita, Café 64 chickpea burger, Explorer's Café salads; no outside food allowed.)

THU NOV 26

Lunch: picnic on Coronado Beach; many restaurants change hours on Thanksgiving. Thanksgiving dinner at your La Mesa stay with the catering trays (Madras Cafe veg trays serve 10 for $79.99; Sattvik's kitchen is all-vegetarian).

FRI NOV 27

Lunch in Julian: Soups & Such Café (vegetarian and vegan options); vegan apple pie from Apple Alley Bakery. Borrego Springs has little vegetarian food, so bring snacks. Dinner: leftovers at home, or Himalayan Cuisine in La Mesa.

SAT NOV 28

Lunch: Royal India in the Gaslamp, about 10 minutes from the Midway (lunch 11–2:30 on weekends; street parking only), or a picnic at Seaport Village. Dinner: San Idli or Dosa Studio in Mira Mesa for a last dosa night (about 20–25 minutes).

SUN NOV 29

Lunch before the flight: Bhojan Griha in Old Town, 10 minutes from the airport, opens 11 AM. Airport food has few clearly vegetarian choices, so pack sandwiches or parathas for the plane.

Packing checklist

Everything to carry for a plane, a van, two desert days, beaches, theme parks and a La Mesa house. Each family can tick items on their own phone; ticks are saved on that device only.

Southwest bags

Free: 1 carry-on (24×16×10 in) and 1 personal item (16.25×13.5×8 in). Checked bags cost $45 first, $55 second on Basic and Choice fares, under 50 lb and 62 in each. Choice Extra, A-List Preferred and Business Select get 2 free; a Southwest credit card gets the first bag free for up to 8 people on the same booking.

Free to check

Car seats, strollers, diaper bags and breast pumps fly free and don't count as bags.

Power banks

Since April 20, 2026, Southwest allows one power bank per person, carry-on only (never in checked bags or overhead bins), and it must stay visible while charging.

Weather

San Diego: about 65–70°F days, 50s at night. Joshua Tree: mid-30s at dawn, about 60°F in the afternoon. Tram summit (8,516 ft): 30s–40s, snow possible.
