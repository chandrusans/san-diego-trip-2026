MON NOV 23

Palm Springs: tram up the mountain

  • 8:00 AMLeave San Diego. About 2 hours to Palm Springs via I-15 and I-10.
  • 10:30 AMPalm Springs Aerial Tramway. A rotating cable car climbs from 2,643 ft to 8,516 ft in about 10 minutes. Short walks and views at the top; it can be snowy.
  • 2:00 PMLunch in Palm Springs, then check in. Yucca Valley is about 45 minutes from the tram and 15 minutes from the park.
  • NightStargazing. The Joshua Tree area is a Dark Sky region; step outside the hotel after dark.
Tram: $36.95 adult / $20.95 child 3–10Tram parking: $15

The top can be about 40°F colder than the valley floor. Bring jackets, hats and gloves for the kids.

Option: one rental house for all three families

A 4–5 bedroom house in Serra Mesa, Clairemont, La Mesa or Allied Gardens (10–20 minutes from everything, with driveways) likely runs about $450–900 a night plus cleaning and service fees for that week, which can beat three hotel suites. You also get a kitchen for Thanksgiving dinner and a washer. Avoid beach neighborhoods: tight street parking and higher prices.

Check before booking: driveway length for the van, real bed count for 6 adults plus kids, number of bathrooms, pool fencing, the all-in total, cancellation policy, and that the listing has a city short-term rental license.

Travel tips

Southwest

Southwest now assigns seats. On Basic fares, families get seats at check-in and may not sit together, so check your seats now. Checked bags now cost extra ($45 first, $55 second); car seats and strollers check free.

The van

A 15-seater is tall. Avoid underground garages (downtown and La Jolla); use open lots. Park nose-out and allow extra stopping distance on mountain roads to Julian and Palm Springs.

Car seats

California requires a car seat or booster for kids under 8 (or under 4'9"). Bring your own or reserve with the van.

Weather

San Diego days are mild (60s–70s °F) and evenings cool. The desert is near freezing at dawn, and the tram summit can be snowy. Pack layers and jackets for everyone.

Thanksgiving week

Mon–Wed are the quiet days; that's why the big parks and Joshua Tree land there. Expect crowds Fri–Sun everywhere.

Fly home day

Return the van by about 2:30 PM and be at the terminal 2.5–3 hours before the 6 PM flight.
