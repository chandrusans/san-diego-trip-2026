Option: one rental house for all three families

A 4–5 bedroom house in Serra Mesa, Clairemont, La Mesa or Allied Gardens (10–20 minutes from everything, with driveways) likely runs about $450–900 a night plus cleaning and service fees for that week, which can beat three hotel suites. You also get a kitchen for Thanksgiving dinner and a washer. Avoid beach neighborhoods: tight street parking and higher prices.

Check before booking: driveway length for the van, real bed count for 6 adults plus kids, number of bathrooms, pool fencing, the all-in total, cancellation policy, and that the listing has a city short-term rental license.

Travel tips

Southwest

Southwest now assigns seats. On Basic fares, families get seats at check-in and may not sit together, so check your seats now. Checked bags now cost extra ($45 first, $55 second); car seats and strollers check free.

The van

A 15-seater is tall. Avoid underground garages (downtown and La Jolla); use open lots. Park nose-out and allow extra stopping distance on mountain roads to Julian and Palm Springs.

Car seats

California requires a car seat or booster for kids under 8 (or under 4'9"). Bring your own or reserve with the van.

Weather

San Diego days are mild (60s–70s °F) and evenings cool. The desert is near freezing at dawn, and the tram summit can be snowy. Pack layers and jackets for everyone.

Thanksgiving week

Mon–Wed are the quiet days; that's why the big parks and Joshua Tree land there. Expect crowds Fri–Sun everywhere.

Fly home day

Return the van by about 2:30 PM and be at the terminal 2.5–3 hours before the 6 PM flight.

Food plan: Indian & vegetarian

Every day has a vegetarian-safe plan. San Diego's Indian hub is Miramar / Mira Mesa, about 20 minutes north of the hotel, where you'll find pure-vegetarian dosa and thali houses and Indian grocery stores. Ratings come from Google or the review site named on each card.

For a group of 15

Call ahead for a table, especially on weekends. Most of these places don't take online bookings for big groups.

Strict vegetarians

Ask about egg in naan and desserts, ghee if you avoid it, and lard or chicken stock in Mexican beans and rice. Pie crusts in Julian can contain lard, so choose the vegan pies.

Theme parks

Zoo: small single-person snacks are allowed (cooler under 12"×12", no glass). SeaWorld: no outside food or picnics, so eat a big breakfast.

Stock up early

On Sunday evening visit an Indian grocery in Miramar for theplas, parathas, khakhra, namkeen, fruit and ready meals. They cover the desert days, where Indian food is scarce.

Day-by-day meals

SAT NOV 21

Late arrival, nothing nearby is open. Pack home-made snacks (theplas, chivda) in carry-on for the first night.

SUN NOV 22

Lunch: Bhojan Griha in Old Town (veg momos, saag paneer, veg biryani). Dinner: Madurai Kitchen, pure-veg South Indian maha thali, then shop at Miramar Cash & Carry, 2 minutes away, for the week's snacks.

MON NOV 23

Lunch on the mountain: Pines Café at the tram top (hummus & falafel, cheese pizza) or Peaks (vegetarian burger, mushroom ravioli). Dinner: Sam's Indian Food in Joshua Tree, or Monsoon in Palm Springs. Buy picnic supplies at Walmart in Yucca Valley (open till 11 PM).

TUE NOV 24

Breakfast: Frontier Café, Yucca Valley (opens 7 AM, vegetarian sandwiches). Lunch: packed picnic inside Joshua Tree; there's no food in the park. Dinner on the drive back: Curry Craft in San Marcos, just off the freeway (veg biryani, chana masala).

WED NOV 25

Zoo: carry small snacks; Lagoon Terrace is the zoo's vegetarian-tagged café. Dinner: Sattvik Foods, Gujarati thali with Jain dishes. Order your Thanksgiving trays here or at Madras Cafe and pick them up tonight.

THU NOV 26

Thanksgiving: many places change hours, so call ahead. Easiest plan: Indian catering trays (Madras Cafe veg trays serve 10 for $79.99) eaten at the hotel or rental house. Lunch near La Jolla: Taste of the Himalayas (vegan items marked).

FRI NOV 27

Lunch in Julian: Soups & Such Café (vegetarian and vegan options); vegan apple pie from Apple Alley Bakery. Borrego Springs has little vegetarian food, so bring snacks. Dinner: San Idli, pure-veg dosas with unlimited sambar.

SAT NOV 28

SeaWorld: Manta Pizza (margherita), Café 64 (chickpea burger) and Explorer's Café salads. Dinner: Himalayan Spice & Savor in Pacific Beach, 10 minutes away, large veg menu and free parking.

SUN NOV 29

Lunch before the flight: Bhojan Griha in Old Town, 10 minutes from the airport, opens 11 AM. Airport food has few clearly vegetarian choices, so pack sandwiches or parathas for the plane.
